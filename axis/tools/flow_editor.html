How to
  • Left-drag empty space: rotate · right-drag: pan · wheel: zoom
  • Drag a point: moves it parallel to the screen. Rotate the view to move it in depth.
  • A point turns yellow when it sits outside the blood space (inside muscle).
  • Del deletes the selected point, A adds one after it.
  • The path starts at the inflow valve and ends at the outflow valve; the app joins it to the vein and artery streams.
